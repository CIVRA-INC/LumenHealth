# Code of Conduct

We follow the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and do not
harass or discriminate. Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainers
through the repository's private contact channel (GitHub Security tab for security matters, or a
maintainer directly for conduct matters). Maintainers may remove comments, commits and
contributions, and may ban contributors, for behaviour that breaks this code.

This policy applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.
